Typography and Text Styling

Exploring fonts, weights, spacing, and text decorations

The Art of Readable Web Text

Typography is one of the most important parts of web design. Choosing the right font family, weight, and size can make content easier to read and more pleasant to look at. Good spacing with line-height and letter-spacing helps guide the eye across each line without strain.

This paragraph uses a medium font weight of 600. Designers often mix light, regular, and bold text to create hierarchy. A highlighted phrase draws attention to key ideas without breaking the flow of reading.

Why Text Styling Matters

Strong emphasis with bold text and soft emphasis with italics give writers tools to shape meaning. Drop caps and first-line styling add a classic print feel to online articles. Balanced alignment and generous line spacing keep long passages comfortable for the reader.

Tip: Use font-weight 300 for soft headings, 600 for subheads, and 700 for titles. Combine them carefully so the page feels clear, not busy.
