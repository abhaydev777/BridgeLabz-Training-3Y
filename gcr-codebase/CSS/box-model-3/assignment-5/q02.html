Kyoto, Japan

Culture Nature Food Plan

A favorite destination

Quiet temples, lively streets, lasting memories.

Kyoto brings together centuries of craft, architecture, gardens, and everyday city life.

Living history

Walk through traditional neighborhoods, visit wooden machiya houses, and discover ceremonies shaped by generations.

Green escapes

From the Arashiyama bamboo grove to the calm paths around the Philosopher's Walk, nature is always close.

Seasonal flavors

Local markets and small restaurants offer ramen, matcha sweets, tofu dishes, and beautifully presented seasonal meals.

Plan a thoughtful visit

Choose a slower pace, use public transport, and leave room for an unexpected street, garden, or tea shop.
